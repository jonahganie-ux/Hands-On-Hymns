Our story · Founded 2024

Many voices.
One purpose.

Hands-On Hymns began with four boys, a song, and a shared desire to keep their eyes on Jesus. This is the story and meaning behind our ministry.

Where it began

A song became
a ministry.

ESTABLISHED2024UNIT 4 · ROOM 31

Hands-On Hymns began in 2024 with four members of the Tema group: Terence (Y3), Junior Yaka (Y2), Clyde Nefoti (Y2), and Moka Gibuma (Y1). They were known as the “Sons Boys” and met in Unit 4, Room 31.

Through togetherness and the leading of God’s Spirit, the idea of singing “Awesome God” unfolded. Their hope was to encourage one another, keep their focus on Jesus, and be missionaries—carrying the motto “Here Am I” (Isaiah 6:8).
